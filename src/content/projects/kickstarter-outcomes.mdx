## Overview

What distinguishes successful crowdfunding campaigns from those that fall short?
This project examines **197,675 Kickstarter projects** to explore how funding
goals, backer support, campaign duration and market context relate to observed
outcomes. A dedicated Hong Kong analysis investigates whether its high success
rate persists when campaigns with comparable categories and funding targets
are examined.

The report combines distribution analysis, comparative charts and interactive
exploration. It emphasizes not only the patterns in the data, but also the
definitions and limitations needed to interpret those patterns correctly.
The source is the Web Robots Kickstarter September 2025 snapshot; the results
describe the available sample rather than the entire crowdfunding market.

## Research questions

1. **Campaign outcomes:** What proportion of campaigns succeed, and how does the treatment of live and canceled projects affect that percentage?
2. **Campaign characteristics:** How do funding goals, observed backer support and campaign duration relate to outcomes?
3. **Categories and trends:** How do project volume, funding and success differ across categories and launch years, and what can platform recognition tell us?
4. **Geographic differences:** Do countries with the largest project samples also have the highest completed-campaign success rates?
5. **Hong Kong:** Does its higher success rate persist within comparable categories and funding-goal bands?

## Interactive report

The report follows these questions in sequence. The principal comparisons are
visible by default; controls allow closer examination of particular categories,
sample-size thresholds and funding measures. Hover over, tap or focus a chart
mark to inspect exact values. Each chart also provides an aggregate data table.

## Visualization decisions

**Aligned panels for different units.** Counts, percentages and monetary
amounts often need to be compared at the same time. The report places them
in aligned panels with the same category order and separate labeled scales.
This removes the need to remember values while switching metrics and avoids
an apparent relationship created by the arbitrary scaling of two overlaid
axes. Annual totals and medians similarly share the same year order, but
retain independent scales.

**A shared axis for the same measure.** The Hong Kong category comparison uses
a dumbbell chart: two differently shaped marks connected on a single
success-rate axis. Readers can compare both the direction and size of the
within-category difference directly.

**Different charts for different questions.** Stacked bars describe project
status composition; histograms and percentile box plots reveal skew and
spread; a treemap provides a funding-share overview; and annual bars and
lines distinguish aggregate totals from typical campaign values. Exact
comparisons remain available through labeled axes, mark details and tables.

**Interaction for investigation.** Filters change the population, sample-size
threshold or analytical focus. They supplement the default comparisons rather
than conceal one half of the evidence. Keyboard access, visible units and
aggregate data tables support readers with different browsing preferences.

## Methodology and limitations

The standardized table contains 197,676 cleaned records. Removing one
duplicated project ID leaves 197,675 unique projects, launched between
April 25, 2009 and September 11, 2025. Funding goals are converted to USD using
the source static exchange rates. Counts and funding summaries use all
project states unless a chart explicitly specifies completed campaigns.

R and Tableau supported the original exploratory analysis. The interactive
web report uses audited aggregate summaries recomputed in Python and
browser-native SVG charts; row-level project data are not included in the
page. The original source files remain unchanged.

The snapshot is not assumed to be a complete census of Kickstarter activity.
Unequal coverage, currency conversion, incomplete recent cohorts and
unobserved campaign characteristics constrain the conclusions. The purpose
of the report is to identify and critically evaluate patterns, not to claim
causal effects or offer guaranteed strategies for campaign success.
